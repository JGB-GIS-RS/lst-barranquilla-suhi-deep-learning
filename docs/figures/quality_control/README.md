# Quality-control figures

This directory contains lightweight diagnostic figures generated by:

```text
notebooks/03_quality_control.ipynb
```

The figures document the spatial and temporal consistency checks applied before
normalization, tensor construction, patch extraction, and model training.

## Documented diagnostics

Depending on the executed notebook outputs, the figures may summarize:

- annual valid-pixel coverage;
- spatial consistency of the study-domain and terrestrial masks;
- differences between annual observation support and the common-valid modeling
  domain;
- annual LST availability and spatial completeness;
- geometry, extent, alignment, resolution, and coordinate-reference consistency;
- anomalous annual products requiring inspection;
- the spatial distribution of excluded water, invalid, or unavailable pixels.

## Interpretation

These figures are quality-control products. They are intended to verify that the
annual raster inputs are sufficiently consistent for downstream modeling.

The validity and terrestrial masks define where loss functions and evaluation
metrics may be computed. They are not explanatory predictors and should not be
interpreted as model inputs.

Differences in annual valid-pixel coverage may reflect clouds, cloud shadows,
sensor availability, water masking, image support, or other observation-quality
constraints. They do not necessarily indicate errors in the LST retrieval or
preprocessing workflow.

The common-valid modeling domain is used to maintain spatial comparability across
the retrospective annual sequence while preserving the rectangular raster and
patch geometry required by the deep-learning workflow.

## Relationship with lightweight audit products

The tabular and machine-readable quality-control records associated with these
figures are stored under:

```text
data/quality_control/
```

These records may include:

- annual file-availability summaries;
- raster-geometry audits;
- valid-pixel statistics;
- common-domain checks;
- mask-area summaries;
- compact CSV and JSON reports.

## Reproducibility boundary

Full-resolution annual rasters, masks, and intermediate quality-control products
are intentionally excluded from GitHub because of their size.

The figures and lightweight audit records support methodological inspection, but
they do not replace the original GeoTIFF inputs required for complete
re-execution.

These diagnostics should not be interpreted as independent validation of LST
accuracy or model performance.
