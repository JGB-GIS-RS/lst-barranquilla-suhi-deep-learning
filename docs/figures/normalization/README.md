# Normalization figures

This directory contains lightweight diagnostic figures generated by:

```text
notebooks/04_train_only_normalization.ipynb
```

The figures support inspection of the normalization stage applied before tensor
construction and model training.

## Documented diagnostics

Depending on the executed notebook outputs, the figures may summarize:

- the distribution of annual LST values before and after global z-score
  standardization;
- robust percentile limits estimated for NDVI, NDMI, NDBI, UI, SAVI, and BSI;
- normalized spectral-index distributions;
- clipping behavior outside the retained robust percentile range;
- annual valid-pixel coverage after normalization;
- consistency of the common terrestrial modeling domain.

## Interpretation

Normalization parameters are estimated from the training portion of the
processing record and are subsequently held fixed when transforming validation
and test data.

For LST, the normalized values are dimensionless model targets. Reported model
errors are later converted back to degrees Celsius using the
experiment-specific inverse transformation.

For spectral indices, robust percentile-based scaling reduces the influence of
extreme values while preserving the relative annual spatial structure. Values
outside the retained percentile interval may be clipped to the configured
normalized range.

The figures are quality-control products. They should not be interpreted as
independent validation of model performance or as evidence that all annual
distributions are statistically identical.

## Reproducibility boundary

Full-resolution normalized GeoTIFFs and other large raster products are not
stored in this repository.

The corresponding lightweight parameter tables and audit records are available
under:

```text
data/normalization/
```

Archived reference-experiment patches may retain historical normalization
records associated with their original construction. The values used for each
reported experiment are preserved in the corresponding training and evaluation
notebooks.
