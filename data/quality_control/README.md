# Quality-control reports

This directory contains lightweight tabular products generated by:

```text
notebooks/03_quality_control.ipynb
```

The files document the availability, geometric consistency, valid-observation
coverage, common-valid domains, and final terrestrial modeling domain of the
annual Landsat-derived products used in the Barranquilla LST/SUHI workflow.

## Purpose

The quality-control stage verifies that annual LST and spectral-index products are
suitable for temporal stacking, normalization, tensor construction, and patch
extraction.

The reports are designed to support inspection of:

- expected file availability;
- annual product completeness;
- common raster geometry and alignment;
- valid-pixel counts and fractions;
- joint validity across variables;
- year-specific common-valid domains;
- the final terrestrial modeling mask;
- missing or inconsistent products.

These reports provide an auditable record of the preprocessing chain without
requiring the full-resolution raster products.

## Temporal and variable scope

```text
Operational period: 2013-2025
Projected CRS: EPSG:32618
Nominal spatial resolution: 30 m
```

The audited annual variables include:

```text
LST, NDVI, NDMI, NDBI, UI, SAVI, BSI
```

Quality-control products also consider the structural land-domain mask and the
effective availability of valid annual observations.

## Included files

### File-availability audits

- `03_qc_v3_file_existence_2013_2025.csv`  
  Historical file-existence audit generated during an earlier quality-control
  stage.

- `03_qc_v6_file_existence_2013_2025.csv`  
  Updated file-existence audit for the harmonized annual product set used in the
  later workflow.

These tables identify expected products, available files, and missing or
inconsistent entries.

### Common-valid-domain audits

- `03_qc_v6_common_valid_by_year_2013_2025.csv`  
  Annual summary of the common-valid domain after intersecting the required
  observation and masking conditions for each year.

- `03_qc_v6_common_valid_by_year_variable_2013_2025.csv`  
  Variable-level valid-pixel statistics by year, allowing inspection of which
  annual products constrain the common-valid domain.

### Final terrestrial-domain summary

- `03_qc_final_land_domain_summary_2013_2025.csv`  
  Summary of the final land-domain mask used to exclude open-water,
  structurally non-land, and invalid areas from normalization, model training,
  and evaluation.

### Workflow summary

- `03_quality_control_summary_2013_2025.csv`  
  Consolidated record of the principal quality-control checks, temporal scope,
  audited variables, and generated outputs.

## Quality-control logic

The final valid-observation domain combines:

- atmospheric screening from `QA_PIXEL`;
- radiometric-saturation screening from `QA_RADSAT`;
- effective availability of surface-reflectance values;
- effective availability of LST;
- common raster geometry and pixel alignment;
- structural land-domain restrictions;
- exclusion of open-water and non-land areas;
- year- and variable-specific validity checks.

A pixel may be geometrically present in an annual raster but excluded from the
modeling domain when one or more required predictors or the target LST are
invalid or unavailable.

## Naming note

The identifiers `v3` and `v6` in several filenames are historical processing
version labels retained for traceability.

They should not be interpreted as:

- separate scientific experiments;
- different model versions;
- alternative public datasets;
- competing quality-control methodologies.

The later `v6` records correspond to the updated harmonized product chain used
for tensor and patch preparation.

## Interpretation limits

The files in this directory document data quality and spatial validity. They do
not contain:

- model-training samples;
- normalization parameters;
- model predictions;
- performance metrics;
- independent validation results;
- full-resolution masks or annual rasters.

The tabular summaries support methodological verification but do not replace the
full-resolution raster masks used during normalization, tensor construction, and
model evaluation.

## Storage policy

This directory is restricted to lightweight CSV quality-control reports.

The following products are intentionally excluded:

- full-resolution annual GeoTIFFs;
- valid-observation masks;
- structural land-domain rasters;
- aligned raster stacks;
- tensors and patch datasets;
- model checkpoints;
- prediction and residual maps;
- temporary processing products.
